# Market Research: Christian Discipleship Publication

## Executive Summary

Kovasz is best positioned as a Hungarian discipleship and formation publication rather than a generic Christian blog. The strongest comparable patterns come from Desiring God and The Gospel Coalition: both combine editorial publishing with robust taxonomy, featured resources, and audience pathways for distinct reader intents.

## What Current Leaders Do

### Desiring God

- Homepage centers a lead resource and a recent-content list.
- Navigation clusters around resource types: Articles, Messages, Books, Podcasts.
- The product strongly emphasizes recurring format brands like Ask Pastor John, Look at the Book, Solid Joys, and Light + Truth.
- Topic discovery is supported through focused topic clusters such as spiritual growth, life issues, and popular topics.
- Their permissions page explicitly allows personal-use translations, while public translation requires permission.

### The Gospel Coalition

- Homepage is broader and more publication-like, with a large content ecosystem.
- Content is organized around Articles, Blogs, Essays, Series, Videos, Podcasts, Courses, Publications, Themelios, and regional editions.
- Filtering is heavily facet-driven: Scripture, Topics, Sections, Authors, Series.
- It supports both mainstream reader browsing and specialized scholarly content via Themelios.
- This is closer to a magazine platform than a single-blog site.

## Market Implications for Kovasz

1. Use a magazine front page with one strong lead article and a supporting latest grid.
2. Keep the audience split inside series and filters, not as the main homepage question.
3. Treat Scripture, topic, and series as the main discovery mechanisms.
4. Offer stable editorial format brands, but keep them simpler than DG’s multiple branded mini-products.
5. Make resources feel curated and practical, not like a dumping ground.

## Positioning Opportunity

Kovasz can occupy a narrower but clearer space:

- more formation-focused than TGC
- more Hungarian and pastoral than DG
- more practical for new believers and leaders than either generic blog or sermons archive
- more scripture-first than a general opinion publication
